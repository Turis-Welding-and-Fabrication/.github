# Contributing to Turi's Repositories

The useful contribution is the one that leaves the next steward with a clearer, truer and more recoverable picture.

## Working rules

1. **Capture before changing.** Preserve working shop knowledge before replacing or simplifying it.
2. **Source evidence first.** Direct observations, original files, photographs, transcripts and machine/runtime evidence outrank later summaries.
3. **Preserve uncertainty.** `Unknown` is better than a confident invention.
4. **Keep authority separate from capability.** Access to a repo, node or API is not business or production authority.
5. **No secrets in Git.** Use the proper credential and data-custody systems.
6. **Make changes attributable.** Say what changed, why, and what evidence supports it.
7. **Prefer bounded, reversible work.** Especially around legacy and production-adjacent systems.
8. **Leave a recovery path.** Another steward should be able to understand and, where appropriate, undo or reproduce the change.

## People and history

Family stories, oral history and human recollection should retain provenance.

Do not silently turn a later summary into the original story. Keep raw source, transcript, human corrections and derived narrative distinguishable.

## Business usefulness

Do not add technology merely because it is interesting.

Prefer changes that make Turi's easier to understand, operate, recover, hand off or improve for the people actually running the shop.
